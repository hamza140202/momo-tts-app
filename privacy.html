Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 21, 2026 | Effective Date: August 21, 2026
🔒 Core Privacy Guarantee: Momo TTS operates on a 100% on-device architecture. Your text inputs, generated audio, voice models, and projects never leave your physical device and are never transmitted to external cloud servers.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Momo TTS does not collect, store, transmit, or monetize personal data:

  • No Text Input Logging: Text entered into the Studio or Dialogue Editor is processed entirely in local memory.
  • No Audio Transmission: Synthesized speech is generated directly via local neural models and stored only on your internal device storage.
  • No User Accounts or Tracking: The application requires no login, email address, phone number, or telemetry trackers.

2. Local Device Storage & Permissions

Momo TTS utilizes local storage solely for application features:

  • Voice Model Storage: Downloaded neural models are stored in the application's sandboxed local data directory.
  • Temporary Audio Cache: Chunks generated during text splitting are saved to a temporary local cache. Users can clear this cache at any time via Settings › Clear Temporary Audio Cache.
  • Projects Database: Saved projects are stored locally using SQLite and can be deleted by the user at any time.

3. Open-Source Technologies & Acknowledgments

Momo TTS utilizes open-source neural acoustic and vocoder technologies operating in local offline inference mode:

  • Sherpa-ONNX (Apache 2.0 License) — On-device neural runtime.
  • Kokoro-82M & Piper VITS (MIT & Open Model Licenses) — Acoustic neural models.
  • eSpeak-ng (GPL v3 / LGPL) — Text phonemization data.

4. Children's Privacy

Momo TTS does not collect any personal data from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out via our GitHub repository: https://github.com/hamza140202/momo-tts-app.
