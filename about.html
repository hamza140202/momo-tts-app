About Momo TTS

Momo TTS was created with a clear mission: to bring state-of-the-art neural speech synthesis directly onto personal hardware, completely free and private forever.

Why On-Device AI Matters

Cloud-based TTS APIs come with high recurring costs, rate limits, internet reliance, and severe privacy concerns. Momo TTS runs the entire synthesis pipeline right on your phone's processor using optimized neural ONNX runtimes.

Key Capabilities

• Smart Sentence Chunking: Split long texts, articles, and essays at natural linguistic pauses without memory crashes.

• Multi-Voice Casting: Create dynamic multi-character dialogues and audio dramas in seconds.

• Zero-Freeze Isolate Architecture: Keep your mobile UI snappy and responsive during intense generation.
